# OUTWISHED 001 — The Second Lamp

> Superseded after the user established that OUTWISHED is a serialized animated story and must not use HEIT. The current episode 1 script is `001-the-cop-knew-the-code.md`.

## Episode purpose

This origin episode must accomplish five jobs without becoming a lore dump:

1. introduce Nico Vale as a present-day direct descendant of Aladdin;
2. explain why the Lamp legally and magically passes to Nico;
3. introduce Veyr as a brilliant rival rather than a servant or generic villain;
4. demonstrate the series contract through one complete fair-play wish duel;
5. end with Veyr ahead `0–1` and Nico beginning the loophole notebook.

The episode follows Phase 1 of the series bible: Nico prepares intelligently, but still learns that exact wording overrides intent.

## Working premise

Nico receives a final auction warning for the last storage locker in his family trust. Beneath a false floor, Aladdin's notebook leads him to a second lamp hidden from history. The Lamp recognizes Aladdin's bloodline and transfers ownership to Nico. Veyr awakens and reveals why Aladdin hid him: Aladdin could not reliably beat him.

Nico uses his first wish to obtain every rule controlling Veyr. He anticipates a storage-overflow trick and a foreign-language trick by enabling cloud search and auto-translation before the wish. Veyr notices one planted weakness that Nico ignores: the translation app shows only `51% confidence`. Veyr supplies a complete, searchable copy in an extinct Djinn court script. Nico receives exactly what he requested, but not something he can reliably read.

## Canon locked by this episode

- Aladdin is Nico's distant direct ancestor.
- History remembers one famous lamp; Aladdin secretly hid a second lamp containing Veyr.
- The second Lamp remains dormant until touched by a verified descendant.
- Ownership passes through the bloodline together with the physical Lamp and notebook.
- One wish is permitted per round.
- Ten Nico wins earn one untwistable Golden Wish.
- Ten Nico losses earn Veyr's freedom.
- The score begins at `NICO 0 — VEYR 0` for the new owner.
- Veyr must fulfill exact words, but may defeat unstated intent.
- The Lamp is the silent referee and does not speak.

## Exact wish

> **NICO:** "Put a complete, searchable copy of every rule controlling you on this phone—without changing or damaging it."

### What Nico blocks

- incomplete rules;
- rules unrelated to Veyr;
- physical destruction or magical alteration of the phone;
- a storage-overflow attack, through cloud search;
- a basic foreign-language attack, through auto-translation.

### What Nico fails to block

He never requires the copy to be readable, understandable, accurately translated, or written in a language he knows.

### Veyr's single execution choice

At `WISH LOCKED`, Veyr chooses an extinct Djinn court script whose legal vocabulary is poorly handled by Nico's translation app. He does not revise the implementation after Nico reveals auto-translation. The `51% confidence` indicator was visible before the lock, so the final reversal introduces no new condition.

## Strategic-turn audit

The episode contains exactly three meaningful strategic turns:

1. **Veyr exploit:** provide the complete rules in Old Djinn court script.
2. **Nico counter:** use pre-enabled auto-translation and cloud search.
3. **Veyr counter-counter:** exploit the visibly unreliable `51%` translation confidence.

No fourth hidden mechanism is introduced.

## Logic and fair-play audit

- **Exact Words:** the file is complete and searchable.
- **Minimum Magic:** Veyr adds only the file and its required indexing; the phone remains intact.
- **No New Conditions:** the translation confidence appears before `WISH LOCKED`.
- **Preparation Is Legal:** Nico enables translation and cloud search before speaking.
- **One Execution:** Veyr chooses Old Djinn court script once and never changes it.
- **One Wish:** no second wish repairs the result.
- **Visible Clue:** `51% confidence` is shown at `10–11s` and copied by Veyr at `12–17s`.
- **Reversible Slapstick:** Nico loses the storage contents and gets carried outside, but retains the inherited Lamp and notebook and suffers no injury.
