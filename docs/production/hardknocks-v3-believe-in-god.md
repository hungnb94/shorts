## Why This Segment
**Multi-Clip Mashup** (ADR-0022) — the pieces that make this comparison work (3 different billionaires' one-line "yes" answers, one of those same billionaires' much longer reflection, and a 2nd billionaire's own follow-up elaboration) are scattered from 238.8s to 522.8s of a 21:37 source; no single 45-60s window contains all of them.

This is a same-channel, different-angle follow-up to `hardknocks_v2` (`docs/production/hardknocks-v2-implied-comparison.md`), reusing the proven **Implied Comparison** hook structure but on a source about faith rather than money. Unlike v2 (where "OTHERS" and "THIS ONE" were different people throughout), this video's PIVOT is a **callback to the same person** who gave the quick HOOK answer — the unnamed veteran-fund investor says "Of course" in under 2 seconds during the HOOK, then his own fuller answer (revealed ~4s later in the edited timeline) turns out to be a genuinely surprising, nuanced take: he says he continues to "wrestle with God," and that accepting everything without question is what would mean he *isn't* growing in his faith. This is closer to `hardknocks_v1`'s single-person reveal device than v2's multi-person device, applied inside a Multi-Clip Mashup rather than a Contiguous VO.

The TEACH section reinforces the pattern with a 2nd, different billionaire (Clint, the healthcare-exit interviewee) also giving a fuller answer than his own HOOK "I sure do." when asked a natural follow-up ("How did you know God was real?") — confirming the "quick answer isn't the whole story" idea isn't a one-off, same reinforcement role as v2's John segment.

Transcript-driven selection: all 8 pieces were chosen from the mlx_whisper word-timestamped transcript (`output/projects/hardknocks/source/npnyOvchXZs_transcript.json`), each cut to exact word boundaries — checked programmatically, max internal gap 0.3s across all 8 pieces (see Known Issues below).

**Tone decision** (from a `/grill-with-docs` grilling session with the user before cutting): kept strictly neutral/observational — the comparison is about answer *length/depth*, not about whether the belief itself is true, and no editorial stance is taken on faith. Scoped to this video only, by explicit user decision; not generalized into a project-wide ADR.

## Hook Formula Applied
- **Frame-0 face/action** (ADR-0017): cold open on two real faces in tight close-up (the investor being interviewed), not a title card — verified via frame extraction (`/tmp/hk3_frames/f_238.82.jpg` at production time).
- **Gap-not-resolved**: the HOOK only shows three quick "yes" answers — it doesn't reveal that one of those same people is about to give a much longer, more complex answer. Full Hook Gate checklist run and passed (see Stage 0 review, all 7 items, before any cutting).
- **Cause+effect not co-named**: neither the hook overlay nor the YouTube title asserts a specific mechanism ("wrestling = closer to God") as fact — both stay at the "how they answer" level, not "why it's true."
- **Payoff timing**: the pivot (investor's real, surprising answer) begins at t=3.96s of the edited timeline (raw, before the 1.03x speed-up — final ~t=3.84s), inside the ~5-10s window (right at the front edge, tighter than v2's ~7s — appropriate since the HOOK here is only 3 one-line answers, not a longer setup).
- **Caption sync (ADR-0018)**: originally kept this source's own burned-in, word-synced yellow captions as-is (same approach as `hardknocks_v1`/`v2`) — but that only worked under the pillarbox crop, which preserved the caption at full width. After switching to hard-crop per ADR-0024, the source's captions are clipped on both edges, so they were replaced with self-authored `drawtext` captions, word-synced from the `npnyOvchXZs_transcript.json` timestamps, positioned within the 1080px hard-crop width and sitting on a permanent opaque bottom band that fully masks the source's own (now-clipped) caption fragment underneath (see Known Issues). The self-authored captions initially still missed this ADR's keyword-color-emphasis requirement (flat white, no highlight) — fixed on a 3rd render: yellow base @68px, keyword runs colored + sized up to 75px per the ADR-0018 addendum (see Known Issues / Post-Production Retro).
- **Cadence (ADR-0016/0018)**: 3 hard cuts land inside the first ~4s (between 3 different people, at 3 different physical locations) — this alone satisfies Stage 0 item 6 without needing to delay any overlay to manufacture a beat.
- **Hook text overlay timing/size**: applied the lesson generalized from `hardknocks_v2`'s 4-round size correction up front rather than re-discovering it — structure captions start at size=76 (within the 64-84px range v2 converged on) and appear at t=0.1s per ADR-0018, frame-verified at multiple timestamps (0.05s, 1.5s, 4.5s, 10s, 35s, 45s, 48s) with no clipping at any of them.
- **Claim-strength flag** (non-blocking): the HOOK answers are qualitative, one-word "yes" responses — not a Money+Number hook. The concrete dollar/company figures appear via the data_viz_overlay stat cards, not in the hook line itself — same pattern as v2.

## Value-Adds (Transformative Gate, ADR-0007 — min 2 required, must be distinct categories)
1. **data_viz_overlay** — stat cards during the HOOK montage: "120 COMPANIES OWNED" (investor), "$460M COMPANY SALE" (Clint), "$5-10M/YR (NFL CAREER)" (Tim Tebow).
2. **this_or_that_overlay** — a dedicated comparison card at the pivot: "1-WORD ANSWER vs 30-SECOND ANSWER", styled and positioned distinctly (lower third, green text) from the HOOK/PIVOT structure captions above it, so it reads as its own value-add rather than a restatement of the narrative captions.

The "SOME GAVE A QUICK YES.../THIS ONE KEPT GOING..." labels are the hook/structure device itself, not counted toward the 2 required value-adds — same scoping decision as `hardknocks_v2`.

Commentary track: the structure captions + stat cards + comparison card + closing CTA ("ALWAYS GROWING DEEPER", drawn from the investor's own words) satisfy ADR-0007 gate item 1. **No TTS was used** — by explicit user decision during the grilling session, to keep every billionaire's own original audio untouched and match the established hardknocks v1/v2 convention (TTS is scoped to the AI-education niche only, per ADR-0021).

## Known Issues
- **Crop convention: hard-crop + self-authored captions (ADR-0024)**: this source's burned-in captions get clipped on both edges by a plain center-crop-to-1080 — confirmed by frame-testing during Stage 0. The *first* render worked around this with the blur-fill pillarbox technique from `render_bacsihai_v6.py` (full uncropped frame, blurred and scaled to fill 1080x1920, sharp footage centered in a scaled-down band). The user reviewed that render and rejected the pillarbox look outright, which produced **ADR-0024** (hard-crop mandatory, pillarbox banned project-wide except grandfathered `bacsihai_v6`). The video was re-rendered same-day: hard center-crop (matching v1/v2) + self-authored `drawtext` captions word-synced from the transcript, sitting on a permanent opaque bottom band that masks the source's own now-clipped caption fragment. See Post-Production Retro below for the two bugs hit while implementing this.
- **Pause-trimming was a no-op again** — checked programmatically: max internal word-gap across all 8 chosen pieces was 0.3s, because each piece was already selected at exact word boundaries during transcript review (same finding as `hardknocks_v2` and `bacsihai_v6`). The mandatory uniform speed-up (1.03x — milder than v1/v2's 1.06-1.1x) was still applied per the base-quality rule; kept mild because the raw 50.4s cut was already right at the ~50s target and a stronger speed-up would have pushed it toward the bottom of the 45-60s range.
- The investor is unnamed in the source video/transcript (no on-screen name card, unlike Tim Tebow who states his own name at t=823s). Referred to only as "the investor" in this doc and in code comments — no name is asserted in any on-screen overlay, so this doesn't affect the video itself.
- **Keyword-emphasis compliance gap (2nd render), fixed on 3rd render**: the self-authored `DIALOGUE_BURSTS` captions added on the 2nd render (for ADR-0024) rendered every burst in one flat white color at 52px, missing ADR-0018's pre-existing "one keyword per burst visually emphasized" rule entirely — this rule already applied project-wide before v3 even started, it was simply not implemented. Caught via a `/grill-with-docs` session, not any automated check. Fixed with a segment-based renderer (`dialogue_burst_filters()`): yellow base @68px, two color-coded keyword vocabularies at 75px (white for core-topic words, `0xFF3B1A` for personal/emotional words), pixel-positioned via Pillow so multi-color/multi-size runs stay aligned on one baseline. Widths were checked programmatically against all 73 bursts beforehand (max line width 896.7px of 1080px frame) — no burst wraps or overflows at the new sizes, confirmed via frame extraction at 8 sample timestamps across all burst shapes (base-only, single-keyword, and mixed-both-groups-in-one-line).

## Post-Production Retro

### Hook Retro
- Verbal: none found — reusing the real "Do you believe in God?" / one-word-answer exchanges directly from the source gave a stronger, more natural hook than anything scripted from scratch would have; no changes made after the first cut.
- Visual: **one found, fixed same-day, pre-upload**. The first render's crop choice (blur-fill pillarbox, same technique as `render_bacsihai_v6.py`) was reviewed by the user and rejected outright — pillarbox reads as a horizontal video shrunk and boxed into a vertical frame, not genuine vertical footage, regardless of technically meeting the 1080x1920 spec. This produced **ADR-0024** (hard-crop mandatory, pillarbox banned project-wide except grandfathered `bacsihai_v6`) and required a full re-render. Beyond the crop swap itself, the size/timing lessons generalized from `hardknocks_v2`'s Stage 3/4 corrections (size=76 starting point, t=0.1s appearance, frame-verify every overlay) still held with no further correction rounds needed on the re-render.
- **Two implementation bugs hit during the re-render, both fixed before finalizing**:
  1. **Unescaped-apostrophe filter-parse crash**: the new `esc()` helper (added for the self-authored dialogue captions) had a no-op replacement — `.replace("'", "'")` instead of replacing with a curly quote — so any caption containing an apostrophe (e.g. "doesn't", "In fact,'s" fragments) broke the ffmpeg filtergraph parser with `Error parsing a filter description around: ,drawtext=text='with God.':...`. Root-caused via a byte-level (`xxd`) comparison against `render_hardknocks_v2.py`'s correct `esc()`, which replaces `'` with the U+2019 curly quote (`'`). Fixed by matching v2's behavior.
  2. **Under-sized caption mask left a ghost visible**: after fixing the crash, the render succeeded but frame extraction revealed the source's own (larger, lower-positioned) burned-in caption still showed through faintly beneath the new self-authored caption box — the per-burst `drawtext` box (`black@0.85`, `boxborderw=28`) didn't fully cover it. Fixed by replacing the per-burst boxes with one permanent full-width `BOTTOM_BAND` (`drawbox`, y=1650, h=270) applied first in the filter chain; had to raise opacity from 0.85 to 0.97 (still a faint ghost) to fully opaque 1.0 before the source caption was completely hidden — confirmed via frame extraction at multiple timestamps.
  Both bugs were caught by the project's mandatory visual self-check discipline (frame-extract and look, don't assume), not by any automated check — consistent with existing precedent (`hardknocks_v2`'s multi-round size correction).
- Visual: **a 2nd found, fixed same-day on a 3rd render**. The self-authored dialogue captions added for the hard-crop fix (above) satisfied ADR-0024 but silently missed ADR-0018 — a rule that already existed project-wide before this video, requiring one keyword per caption burst to be visually emphasized in a distinct color. Every burst had rendered in flat white instead. Caught by the user reviewing the 2nd render via another `/grill-with-docs` session, cross-referenced against ADR-0018's own text rather than treated as a fresh design question. Fixed by classifying each burst's words into a base class (yellow, 68px) and two keyword classes (white / `0xFF3B1A`, 75px — the +10% size bump was the user's explicit addition beyond what ADR-0018's original evidence documented, now flagged as an unvalidated extension in that ADR's addendum), then rendering each color/size run as its own `drawtext` filter, pixel-positioned with Pillow so mixed-size runs share one baseline. Frame-verified across single-keyword, mixed-two-keyword, and no-keyword burst shapes — all aligned correctly, no line exceeded ~83% of frame width.
- One idea for a future iteration (not yet applied): this source has many more billionaires answering the same God question beyond the 3 used here (at least Tim Tebow's own much longer apologetics answer, ~845-887s, unused in this cut) — a future video from this same source could build a 2nd, distinct video around Tebow's own longer answer as the PIVOT instead, without reusing any of this video's exact pieces (would need a fresh dedup check against this video's own piece list, not just the source video ID, if attempted).
- Did not run the `viral-video-analysis` skill against a comparison video for this retro (no metrics yet to compare against, and the most relevant comparison — `hardknocks_v2` — is itself still pending metrics) — will fold into the 48h check instead.

### Workflow Delta
Yes — two cases not fully covered by the current docs, both now fixed at the project-policy level:
1. **Crop policy gap, now fixed via ADR-0024**: `docs/WORKFLOW.md` Stage 2 described center-crop as the hardknocks default without stating what to do when a source's captions would be clipped by it. The first render reached for the pillarbox fallback used in `bacsihai_v6`, which the user then rejected as a general technique — not just wrong for this video, but banned for all future Clip Curation Edits. **ADR-0024** now makes the answer explicit: always hard-crop, and when a source's own captions would be clipped, self-author replacement captions from the transcript instead of falling back to pillarbox. `docs/WORKFLOW.md` Stage 2/3 should be read per this ADR going forward.
2. **Caption-authoring implementation gap**: this was the first video where hardknocks needed self-authored (not source-derived) captions. Two bugs surfaced in the process — an unescaped-apostrophe filter crash and an under-sized/insufficiently-opaque caption mask (both detailed in Hook Retro above). Neither is a new rule so much as confirmation that the existing "frame-verify every overlay, don't assume" discipline (ADR-0018) catches this class of bug reliably when actually followed.
3. **Compliance gap, now fixed via ADR-0018 addendum**: switching to self-authored captions (item 2 above) reintroduced ADR-0018's keyword-color-emphasis rule as something the render script itself must implement, rather than something inherited for free from the source's own captions — and the first version of that implementation missed it. The fix is now the reference implementation (`dialogue_burst_filters()` in `render_hardknocks_v3.py`) for any future self-authored dialogue captions, recorded as an ADR-0018 addendum rather than a video-scoped experiment, per the user's explicit choice. Worth flagging for future self-authored-caption videos: don't just satisfy the crop/legibility constraint (ADR-0024) and assume caption-styling rules (ADR-0018) come along for free — check both explicitly.
4. Everything else about this production followed `docs/WORKFLOW.md` Stage 0-6 without needing a new rule or process change — the grilling-session decisions (sub-format, tone, commentary track, value-add types) were all made explicitly before Stage 1 began, so no mid-production surprises occurred there.
