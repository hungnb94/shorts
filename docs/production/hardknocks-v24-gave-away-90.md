## Fact-Check Boundary

The interview says the company sold for `$1.5 billion`. Nestlé's official October 30, 2020 announcement is more precise:

- Freshly was valued at `$950 million`;
- potential earnouts were up to `$550 million`;
- those earnouts were contingent on successful business growth;
- Freshly's 2020 forecast sales were `$430 million`.

Therefore the video may say `A $1.5B HEADLINE`, `UP TO $1.5B`, or `$950M + UP TO $550M`. It must not present the entire `$1.5B` as unconditional cash paid at closing.

Primary source:

- <https://www.nestle.com/media/pressreleases/allpressreleases/nestle-usa-acquires-freshly>

The founder's claimed personal ownership, bank balance and debt remain clearly attributed interview statements. The video will not state his personal payout as a verified fact.
