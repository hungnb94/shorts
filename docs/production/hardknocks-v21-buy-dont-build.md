## Executive Decision

Use School of Hard Knocks source `QJP9OXmI5_4`, not another billionaire-origin montage. The transferable winner from V17 is a source-native, quantified decision that lets the viewer choose a side immediately. The single innovation imported from `-uHCTHfgBco` is **Three-Layer Proof Loop**:

- CONTEXT answers **who is in what situation?**
- DECISION answers **what move changes the state?**
