# Capytech Higgsfield v5 — Budget Production Strategy

Date: 2026-07-28
Status: approved by user direction to use Higgsfield economically; production in progress

## Original v1 review

The exact original reviewed was `output/projects/capytech/final/2026-07-27-capytech_v1_charging_eras.mp4` plus its fresh contact sheet and QC record.

### What already works

- One instantly readable objective: charge the same phone from 1%.
- Three escalating eras: easy 2000 → annoying 2026 → dangerous 2050.
- The 2050 reversal is a real payoff: the phone charges by draining the character.
- The system update returns the phone to 1%, creating a clean loop.
- Technical baseline is sound: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, 55 seconds, continuous motion and safe loudness.

### Weaknesses to fix

1. The contact sheet repeats almost the same locked wide composition for several consecutive frames. The story changes, but the camera rarely does.
2. The procedural block character is readable but does not provide the premium, expressive 3D performance expected from a high-retention entertainment Short.
3. The hook shows urgency but does not frame the scene as a large challenge with a withheld losing year.
4. The 2026 sequence explains friction mainly with interface text; physical adapter comedy needs to carry the information.
5. The CTA is a generic panel rather than a consequence inside the story.
6. The original QC JSON contains contradictory provenance/manual fields, so v5 must assign PASS only from one fail-closed set of gates.

## Upgraded viral story

The same original capybara protagonist, Volt, gets the same phone at 1% in 2000, 2026 and 2050. The storytelling uses challenge, visible stakes, escalating obstacles, scoreboard progression and a large consequence without copying any creator's likeness, wording, music or proprietary assets.

Hook promise:

`THREE YEARS. ONE PERCENT BATTERY. ONE CHARGER DRAINS HIM TO ZERO.`

Story progression:

1. Hook: Volt lunges for an escaping cable; the losing year stays hidden.
2. 2000: one enormous charger, one plug, instant 100%; 2000 takes the lead.
3. 2026: wrong connector → adapters multiply → cable remains short → forced update.
4. 2050: AI orb promises effortless charging; Volt accepts; scan reveals `ENERGY SOURCE: YOU`.
5. CTA: the orb locks at 99%; Like, Subscribe and Comment `CHARGE` power three circuits.
6. Payoff: phone reaches 100%; Volt reaches 0% and falls safely onto a padded charging mat.
7. Loop: system update drains the phone back to 1% and restores the opening composition.

## Cost-efficient generation topology

Generate only five premium motion shots, then create the requested 1–3 second final cuts through temporal subranges, punch-ins, prop macros and local UI animation.

| Shot | Generated action | Final editorial use |
|---|---|---|
| G01 Hook | cable escapes; Volt lunges and reacts | 4 hook cuts plus loop reprise |
| G02 2000 | heavy charger drops; plug; success | 3 action cuts plus scoreboard |
| G03 2026 | wrong port; adapter chain; short cable; update | 6 obstacle cuts |
| G04 2050 | orb offer; accept; scan; energy link | 6 reveal/drain cuts |
| G05 Payoff | completion pulse; Volt drains and collapses | 3 payoff cuts |

The final EDL remains the 29-cut, 50.5-second schedule in `output/projects/capytech/scripts/capytech_higgsfield_v4_storyboard.json`. Every cut is 1.0–2.3 seconds. Twenty-five seconds come from unique Higgsfield motion; 25.5 seconds come from moving close-ups, reframes, scoreboards, meters, CTA circuits and the intentional hook-loop reprise. No static hold or non-uniform stretch is allowed.

## Final gates

- Exactly one Volt in every generated shot.
- Full-length coral trousers remain visible and stable whenever the lower body appears.
- Same face, body proportions, wardrobe, phone and room logic across adjacent shots.
- No generated text, logo, watermark, extra limb or body fusion.
- Every final cut is 1.0–3.0 seconds and changes story information or camera scale.
- Caption visible by 0.2 seconds; early SFX by 1.0 second; diegetic Triple CTA starts at 38.0 seconds.
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo, 50.5 seconds.
- Full decode, detectors, final ASR, loudness, contact sheet, hook sheet, CTA/payoff frames and generative continuity review must all pass on the exact final MP4.
- External naive-viewer Hook Gate and ADR-0035 lane eligibility remain unverified; no upload is authorized.
