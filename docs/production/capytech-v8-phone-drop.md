## Source-format contract

One hero phone is repeatedly dropped onto one spring impact plate while one scalar changes: height (`1cm`, `1m`, `10m`, `100m`). A telescoping measuring tower communicates that scalar. Four compact result silhouettes record damage without introducing additional hero phones.

The scene and production-specific rig live in `pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py`. It creates:

- a high-key 1080x1920 Blender 5.2 studio;
- one animated camera using impact-macro, character-medium and tower-scale shot families;
- five pose families: `ready_release`, `smug`, `wince_inspect`, `shock_brace`, `panic_airborne`;
- a visibly compressing and rebounding spring plate;
- event-bound, procedurally synthesized 48 kHz stereo audio;
- a sequential LIKE/SUB/COMMENT CTA from 38.2 through 40.0 seconds while the airborne payoff continues;
- the final spring rebound, destroyed-phone result, dazed reaction and 1cm loop reset.

## Commands

Run from the repository root:

```bash
# Critical exact scene stills, no animation render
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --preview --hook H1
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --preview --hook H3

# Native 1080 benchmark frames at 16, 32 and 64 samples
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --benchmark

# Resumable inclusive frame range
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --frames 1:300 --hook H1 --samples 32

# Explicitly invalidate generated bytes after renderer/storyboard/sample changes
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --reset-cache --hook H1 --frames 1:90 --samples 32

# Render H1/H3 hook frames, then encode their first three seconds
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --frames 1:90 --hook H1 --samples 32
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --encode-hook --hook H1
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --frames 1:90 --hook H3 --samples 32
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py --encode-hook --hook H3

# Full H1 render, procedural audio and exact-final ffmpeg mux
python3 pipeline/capytech/render_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py

# Fail-closed exact-final QC
python3 pipeline/capytech/verify_capytech_v8_phone_drop.py
```

Blender must resolve on `PATH` and report version 5.2. ffmpeg and ffprobe must also resolve on `PATH`. The default full command renders all 1,545 native PNG frames, encodes H.264 video, synthesizes and normalizes original audio, then muxes the exact final. Range renders resume only when renderer SHA-256, storyboard SHA-256, hook, samples, Blender version, dimensions, FPS and frame count match the cache identity; otherwise they fail closed and require `--reset-cache`.

## Benchmark decision

Benchmark mode rendered representative native 1080 frames at 16 and 32 samples. The 16-vs-32 comparison measured mean absolute pixel differences of 1.48-1.88/255 across five critical frames, but native visual inspection still found cleaner edge/shadow stability at 32. The final render therefore uses 32 samples; 64 was not selected because it adds render cost without evidence of a meaningful mobile-scale gain for this flat high-key Eevee scene.

Both exact 3.000-second hook MP4s were rendered at native 1080x1920/30 fps. H1 scored 49/60 and H3 scored 43/60 on Curiosity Gap, Specificity, Visual, Emotion, Payoff and Novelty. H1 is selected because it preserves a clear direct-action contract. H3 was rejected because its `100m!` flash-forward collided with `DROP 1 cm`, weakened temporal coherence and leaked the rebound payoff. Evidence is recorded in `output/projects/capytech/analysis/v8_phone_drop_qc/hook_gate.json`.

## Exact-final verification

The verifier exits non-zero for every failed objective or semantic gate. It:

- probes exact duration, dimensions, CFR, codecs, pixel format and audio layout;
- fully decodes audio and video;
- runs black, freeze and silence detectors;
- measures integrated loudness and encoded true peak;
- extracts exact-final semantic frames for six roles in every round: pre-release, airborne, contact, damage/result, reaction extreme and rail update;
- extracts the three 100m scale states, compression-before-rebound, rebound, final damage, final face and loop reset;
- creates exact-final semantic and reduced 270x480 pose-family sheets;
- creates five dense sequence sheets at 0.25-second intervals for hook, compression/rebound, airborne CTA, payoff and loop;
- binds evidence and renderer manifests to the exact-final SHA-256;
- rejects stale evidence;
- reports pixel/footer/clipping measurements as diagnostics only.

Semantic content is never passed from generic motion. On first run, the verifier creates `semantic_assessment.json` with all semantic fields false and exits non-zero. A reviewer must inspect the generated exact-final frames and five dense sequence sheets, keep the exact artifact/evidence hashes, identify the reviewer/date, and provide substantive notes for every item, sequence window and global gate covering pose readability, eye-line, penetration, causal ordering and layout integrity. Rerendering changes the hash and invalidates that assessment.

The five-person cold-viewer hook gate remains `UNVERIFIED` and is reported separately. It does not make a technically and semantically verified local artifact fail, but it must not be represented as passed without five real independent responses.

## Post-production retro

The first Codex implementation audit stopped an in-progress render after finding three fail-closed defects: unversioned frame reuse, CTA at 27.2s instead of the mandatory 38-42s window, and detector failures able to look like passes. The renderer now binds cache identity to renderer/storyboard/hook/samples/Blender/spec, the CTA runs at 38.2-40.0s over airborne motion, and detector/loudness errors are blocking. Codex re-audit returned GO for the clean render.

Exact-final sequence review then caught world-space result cards enlarging into the loop title after 49.2s. That artifact was rejected, the rail was cleared before reset, the cache was invalidated, and all 1,545 frames were rendered clean again. Final objective results: 1080x1920 H.264/yuv420p, 30fps CFR, 51.500s video and audio streams, 1,545 decoded frames, AAC 48kHz stereo, -16.44 LUFS, -1.59 dBTP, full decode pass, and zero black/freeze/silence detector events. The final verifier exited zero with no failed gates.

Human cold-viewer evidence remains unavailable and virality remains unproven. Local QC improves readiness; it does not justify a prediction that the video will receive millions of views.
