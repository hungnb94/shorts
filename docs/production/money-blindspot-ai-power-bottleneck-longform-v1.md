## 1. Strategic Decision

### Selected topic

**AI's real bottleneck is not only chips — it is electricity infrastructure, especially grid connections and transformers.**

### Why this wins now

| BNPL hidden economics | 15 | 19 | 16 | 17 | 8 | 5 | 80 |

The AI/grid topic combines a large current attention pool with a narrower, under-covered causal mechanism. The 2026 IEA update reports that AI-factory capacity more than tripled in 18 months, data-centre electricity use grew 17% in 2025, and AI-focused data-centre electricity use grew 50%. The business mechanism remains evergreen: every compute cycle depends on generation, transmission, transformers and a grid connection.

The broad YouTube query `AI data center power problem` is competitive, but the narrower `AI transformer shortage data centers` query is materially less saturated. The exact public search snapshot is saved at `output/projects/ai-power-bottleneck/source/youtube-competition-2026-07-28.json`. A second title-level snapshot at `output/projects/ai-power-bottleneck/source/title-competition-2026-07-28.json` found a few emerging micro-competitors, but no established exact-title incumbent; this is low established competition, not zero competition. The opportunity is not another generic “AI uses lots of power” explainer; it is a causal, business-focused transformer and interconnection story.

### Blue-ocean wedge

Generic competitors discuss AI chips, emissions or nuclear power as separate stories. MONEY BLINDSPOT will audit the whole commercial stack:

`AI demand → data centre → grid connection → transformer → firm power → who earns / who bears the risk`

The video does not recommend stocks. It gives the viewer a reusable decision framework for evaluating AI infrastructure announcements.

## 2. Audience and Promise

Viewer promise:

Payoff object:

> The scarce asset is not just a GPU. It is a deliverable megawatt: generation, grid connection, transformer and contract, all arriving on the same date.
