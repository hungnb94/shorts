## Hook Retro

### Hypothesis

A source-native Money+Number sequence should front-load the strongest contradiction—`>$100M`, age 30, disbelief—before explaining what the company does. This is a production hypothesis, not a causal retention claim.

### Mechanical result

- `$100M` is spoken in the first 1.8s;
- age 30 lands by 3.8s;
- host disbelief lands by 5.2s;
- faces remain visible through the protected hook window;
- first full-screen evidence starts at 10.194s.

### Main analytics risk

The jump from age disbelief to `I sell robots` is intentionally abrupt. If viewers interpret it as a context break rather than escalation, retention may dip around 5–7s. Inspect that slope before reusing this ordering.
