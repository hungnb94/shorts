## Production decision

V10R revises the same robot-founder story with the School of Hard Knocks Live-Approach grammar found in the three-Short evidence review. The user locked four constraints during the production grill:

1. Original interview voice only; no TTS.
2. No face inset or picture-in-picture.
3. Open visually on the host, then cut to the driver, then show the G-Wagon as status proof.
4. Final duration must be at least 45 seconds.
