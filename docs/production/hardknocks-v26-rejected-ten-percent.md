## Why This Format

- The opening is a source-native moving-face contradiction with concrete money and ownership numbers.
- It transfers the proven School of Hard Knocks money/number pattern without reusing the subject, source ID or mechanism of V17–V25.
- The visible progression is causal: rejected stake → player scale → supplier reveal → source-reported valuation → dilution guardrail → independent distribution proof → verdict.
- The signature moment is the viewer decision at frame 0 and the later flip from `casino` to `supplier reaching thousands of storefronts`.

## Post-Production Retro

### What worked

- The source-native failed-raise contradiction produced a moving-face hook with exact money and equity stakes, while the `77M` counter delivered proof before the first mechanism reveal.
- The supplier-versus-casino flip created a real information progression rather than decorative editing.
- Speaker-reported valuation labels, dilution guardrail and AWS checkpoint prevented the `$5B` claim from being mistaken for an audited return.
- Segment-local ASR produced reliable word timing across noisy interview cuts; exact-final ASR retained every required story and CTA token.
- Event-ledger SFX stayed measurable above the bed without masking dialogue.

### What failed and changed

- Whole-timeline Whisper replaced an intelligible 11.2-second source answer with ellipses. The caption workflow now transcribes each hard-cut audio segment separately and offsets the resulting bursts into the final timeline.
- `mlx_whisper` emitted `$100` and punctuation-attached `,000` as separate tokens. Grouping could split them across captions. The builder now merges no-leading-space continuation tokens before applying burst word-count limits.
- The first Codex review scored `97/100`: CTA and AWS secondary labels were too small, and the last sampled frame did not hold the complete payoff. Round 2 enlarged those labels and held `ONE SUPPLIER → THOUSANDS OF STOREFRONTS`, raising the exact-final score to `98/100`.
- Enforcing the strict 2–5-word caption baseline by merging six singleton tails created two long-line overflows. The round-3 fix wraps long 4–5-word bursts across two safe lines and hard-fails if either line exceeds 500px on the 540px overlay canvas. Final Codex verification scored `99/100`.
- A filename/search-result match is not a semantic asset gate: `server_racks_16971219.mp4` was actually warehouse footage. The final renderer now removes that dependency entirely and generates a truthfully labeled infrastructure illustration. Stronger information-bearing rack/scan motion eliminated initial freeze-detector events, while a brighter network floor kept the no-black-footer gate valid.

### Next experiment

- Keep the source-native viewer challenge as the single experiment layer. Do not attribute future performance to the entire production bundle.
- If distribution stalls, test a different source-native hook mechanism on the next eligible lane; do not remove this artifact or republish it simultaneously.
