Three dollar profit per sticker. Not life-changing money.

But then she found TikTok. And everything changed.

She posted ONE video of her drawing process. 3 million views overnight.

Her sticker shop exploded. 5,000 orders in a week. Then 10,000.

By age 18, she had sold over 400,000 stickers. That's $1.2 million in profit.

From her bedroom. With zero employees. Zero inventory. Zero business degree.

Her secret? She didn't sell stickers. She sold identity.
