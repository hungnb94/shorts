You think rich people walk into a store and pay sticker price? Never.

Here's the secret the wealthy use every single day.

First, they negotiate EVERYTHING. Phone bills, gym memberships, even doctor visits.

A quick call asking "Can you do better?" saves them thousands per year.

Second, they buy assets, not liabilities. That Rolex? It actually goes UP in value.

Your $300 sneakers? Worth $30 the moment you walk out of the store.

Third, they use OTHER people's money. Business loans, credit card points, investor cash.

Why spend your own money when someone else will fund it?

Fourth, they wait. The rich don't impulse buy. They wait 72 hours before any purchase over $100.

Most desires disappear in 24 hours. The rich know this.

The difference between rich and broke isn't income. It's behavior.
