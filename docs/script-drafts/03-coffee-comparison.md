A $1 cup of coffee. A $50 cup. And a $1,000 cup. What's actually different?

The $1 coffee? Instant. Hot water. Freeze-dried crystals. Costs about 3 cents to make.

The $50 cup? This is where it gets interesting.

It's called Geisha coffee from Panama. Grown at 1,700 meters altitude.

Hand-picked. Sun-dried. Brewed with precision instruments that look like lab equipment.

The taste? Flora, jasmine, bright acidity. It's genuinely delicious.

But the $1,000 cup? This one's insane.

It's called Black Ivory. Made in Thailand from coffee beans eaten and... excreted by elephants.

Yes. Elephant poop coffee. 33 pounds of beans for 1 pound of usable coffee.

Does it taste better? Maybe. Is it worth $1,000? Absolutely not.

The lesson? Price and value are completely different things.
