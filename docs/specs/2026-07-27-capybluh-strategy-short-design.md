## Chosen Approach

Create an original procedural 2.5D block-character animation. The character uses an original silhouette and non-Capybluh palette. No Capybluh footage or proprietary model is included in the final artifact.

This approach is preferred over generative 3D because it costs nothing, preserves character and prop continuity, gives frame-accurate control over hook timing, and avoids blind paid retries. It is preferred over a commentary remix because it materially reduces copyright and reused-content risk.

## Story Premise

A blocky character tries to save a phone at 1% battery. Charging becomes progressively more absurd across 2000, 2026, and 2050. In 2050, an AI charging orb advertises free charging but secretly selects the human as the energy source. The phone reaches 100%, the human collapses, and a system update drains the phone back to 1%, creating a visual loop.

## Timeline

Target duration: 56–60 seconds.

1. 0.0–3.0s — Hook: frightened face, phone at 1%, charger visibly out of reach, active lunge, alarm SFX within the first second, and `HOW BAD DOES 2050 GET?` visible by 0.2s.
2. 3.0–14.0s — 2000: durable brick phone, one oversized cable, immediate successful charge, compact baseline payoff.
3. 14.0–29.0s — 2026: wrong cables, adapter sold separately, cable barely too short, then a forced update interrupts apparent success.
4. 29.0–47.0s — 2050: floating AI charging orb, `FREE CHARGE*`, scan, accept action, and reveal `ENERGY SOURCE: YOU`.
5. 39.0–42.0s — Diegetic Mid-Roll Triple CTA: the orb pauses at 99% and says, `Like, subscribe, and comment CHARGE to continue.`
6. 47.0–54.0s — False victory: phone reaches 100%; character loses color and collapses at 0% human energy.
7. 54.0–59.0s — Twist and loop: `SYSTEM UPDATE` drains the phone from 100% to 1%; composition resolves near the opening state.

## Visual System

- 9:16, 1080×1920 final output, 30 fps.
- Original rounded block character: copper/orange head and arms, dark navy torso, turquoise legs, white shoes.
- Bright readable rooms and props, shallow pseudo-perspective, soft shadows, exaggerated facial expressions, squash-and-stretch, camera punch-ins, and foreground wipes.
- Era labels are large and persistent during each phase; 2050 is red to signal danger.
- Caption and hook text use Komika Axis with 2–5 words per burst and one emphasized keyword.
- Reserved geometry keeps captions, phone UI, CTA, and moving watermark from colliding.
- One brief Pexels server/power insert may appear inside the 2050 hologram, labeled `ILLUSTRATION`; it is optional and must be rejected if semantically weak or licensing evidence is incomplete.

## Audio System

- No narrative dialogue except the robotic CTA line, maximizing global comprehension.
- Three score states: playful analog, frantic digital, ominous futuristic.
- Event-bound SFX for alarm, plug, cable failures, box opens, scan, accept, energy drain, collapse, and update.
- No constant effect bed. No silence gap of 0.2s or longer unless narratively intentional.
- Final encoded loudness and true peak are measured after AAC encoding.
