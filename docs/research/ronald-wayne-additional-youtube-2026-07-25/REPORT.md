## Copyright / footage risk

- **No candidate in this set exposes a public Creative Commons license.** All footage should be presumed copyrighted.
- **Highest risk:** Insider/Tech Insider, CNN, Bloomberg, CBS archive inside ColdFusion, and the `kV...` Bloomberg reupload. News publishers commonly fingerprint footage; reuploading from an unofficial channel adds provenance risk rather than reducing it.
- **Layered-rights traps:** ColdFusion embeds CBS; `ViF...` appears syndicated/reuploaded broadcast material; `kV...` is a third-party upload of Bloomberg. Avoid these copies as masters.
- **Potentially permission-friendly:** Vintage Computer Federation (`w8...`), TheFirstAppleBook (`mFt...`), roompass/El Club del 1% (`Qw...`), and possibly the Vegas Slots Wayne series (`8r...`) if uploader ownership is confirmed. Contact the publisher for explicit permission/licensing.
- **Transformative-use hygiene:** keep each real-voice quote only as long as needed (1–2.5s), retain the TTS analytical spine, show source ID/on-screen citation, reframe/annotate, and do not lift music beds or extended B-roll. Multiple tiny quotations may strengthen transformation but do **not** guarantee fair use or prevent Content ID claims.
- **Do not crop solely to erase logos.** A reframe for 9:16 is legitimate; obscuring provenance can look worse in a dispute. Preserve internal source records and use a visible citation overlay.

## Editorial accuracy corrections

1. The familiar “sold 10% for $800” line is disputed by Wayne himself in the 2026 interview. The strongest added primary source instead separates: withdrawal/name removal → an $800 check → later $1,500 release of remaining connection.
2. “12 days” should be attributed to Wayne; Wozniak has publicly questioned the literal duration.
3. Auction figures conflict across YouTube sources: direct Wayne interviews say ~$1.3m; a later explainer says ~$1.6m. Use “over $1m” until the auction-house record is checked.
4. The deeper motivation is not only liability: Wayne repeatedly says he believed Apple would succeed but did not want a documentation/back-office identity under Jobs and Wozniak.
