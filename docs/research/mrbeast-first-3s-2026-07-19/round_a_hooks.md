# Round A — Baseline Hook Frameworks (HardKnocks vertical, generic, English)

Each entry is a concrete t=0-3s mini-script for a generic "self-made entrepreneur interview"
subject (placeholder — not tied to any specific footage), built from rules already documented
in this project: gap/mystery must not be resolved by the hook line (CONTEXT.md → Hook,
AGENTS.md), frame-0 must show a human face/action not a title card (ADR-0017), caption visible
by t≤0.2s updating every 1-2s with one keyword emphasized (ADR-0018), and the 5 known Source
Channel Patterns (Money+Number, Curiosity Gap "THIS", Contrarian Reveal, Live-Approach,
Implied Comparison — CONTEXT.md).

---

## A1 — Money+Number Cold Open
**Pattern**: Money+Number
- **Visual (t=0)**: hard cut straight into subject's face mid-answer, no branded card, no title.
- **On-screen text (≤0.2s)**: `"$14,000,000 / YEAR"` bold white, pops in sync with the number being spoken.
- **VO/subject audio**: subject mid-sentence: *"...and last year alone the company cleared fourteen million—"* (cut before the sentence resolves how/why)
- **Gap withheld**: how he got there / what he does. Body section answers it.

## A2 — Curiosity Gap ("THIS")
**Pattern**: Curiosity Gap
- **Visual (t=0)**: subject's face, mid-motion (turning to camera or gesturing), no static shot.
- **On-screen text**: `"THIS is the mistake that almost bankrupted him"` — capitalized deictic, no antecedent given.
- **VO**: subject: *"...and that one decision nearly cost me everything."*
- **Gap withheld**: what "this" refers to — revealed at 6-8s.

## A3 — Contrarian Reveal
**Pattern**: Contrarian Reveal
- **Visual (t=0)**: subject face, calm/serious tone, direct eye contact with interviewer/camera.
- **On-screen text**: `"HARD WORK ISN'T WHY HE'S RICH"`
- **VO**: subject: *"Everyone thinks it's about working harder than everyone else. It's not."*
- **Gap withheld**: the real cause — named later in body.

## A4 — Live-Approach Interruption
**Pattern**: Live-Approach
- **Visual (t=0)**: handheld camera moving toward subject, real interruption ("Excuse me — can I ask you something?"), subject turns mid-stride.
- **On-screen text**: none at t=0 (motion + real reaction carries it); first caption burst lands ≤1s: `"STOPPED HIM AT HIS OWN OFFICE"`
- **VO**: host, off-camera, breathless: *"Sir, real quick — how much is that watch?"*
- **Gap withheld**: subject's reaction/answer, and why the watch matters.

## A5 — Implied Comparison
**Pattern**: Implied Comparison
- **Visual (t=0)**: subject answering a question on camera, ordinary setting, no reveal yet.
- **On-screen text**: `"OTHERS SAID IT WAS IMPOSSIBLE"` — labels only one side, white/yellow.
- **VO**: subject: *"People told me this business would never work."*
- **Gap withheld**: title/genre implies a "...vs THIS ONE" payoff arriving later (~30-40s tolerance per Implied Comparison's confirmed pattern); no on-screen text resolves it early.

## A6 — Blindspot Scorecard Tease
**Pattern**: new — adapted from V11's Blindspot Verification structure into a hook (not just a body format)
- **Visual (t=0)**: subject's blurred freeze-frame face, "?/3" panel forming on screen.
- **On-screen text**: `"1. WEALTH  2. FREEDOM  3. LEGACY — DOES HE HAVE ALL 3?"`
- **VO**: narrator: *"Every self-made millionaire claims all three. Let's check."*
- **Gap withheld**: the score — resolved only at the end (44-59s in V11's case).

## A7 — Interrupted-Answer (no visible question)
**Pattern**: hybrid Curiosity Gap / Contrarian
- **Visual (t=0)**: subject already mid-answer, camera doesn't show the host's question at all.
- **On-screen text**: `"HIS ANSWER SURPRISED EVERYONE"`
- **VO**: subject: *"...no. Actually, the opposite happened."*
- **Gap withheld**: what the actual question/topic was.

## A8 — Number-Then-Correction
**Pattern**: Money+Number + Contrarian hybrid
- **Visual (t=0)**: bold number overlay appears first, subject's face visible underneath (not a title card — face stays on screen, ADR-0017 compliant).
- **On-screen text**: `"$50,000,000"` → strikethrough animates in at ~1.5s → `"...ACTUALLY $200,000,000"`
- **VO**: subject: *"Everyone quotes the wrong number."*
- **Gap withheld**: why the real number is different / what it reveals about him.

## A9 — Rank/Prediction Tease
**Pattern**: new — invites active viewer prediction (game-like, similar mechanic to MrBeast-style dare/challenge per hook-benchmarks-2026-07 finding)
- **Visual (t=0)**: 3 quick face-cuts (0.3s each) of 3 different subjects, then freeze on the 3rd.
- **On-screen text**: `"3 BILLIONAIRES. ONE LIED."` on the freeze frame.
- **VO**: narrator: *"Can you guess which one?"*
- **Gap withheld**: which one, and what the lie was.

## A10 — Physical-Prop Reveal
**Pattern**: new — object-first cold open (no number spoken yet, prop stands in for it)
- **Visual (t=0)**: extreme close-up on an object (car key fob, a check, a watch clasp) filling the frame, then a hard pull-back/whip-pan reveals the subject's face at ~0.8-1.2s (face still lands inside the ≤2s hook window).
- **On-screen text**: none at t=0; first burst at ~1s: `"THIS KEY OPENS A $2M GARAGE"`
- **VO**: subject, already talking as the pull-back happens: *"...so this one right here—"*
- **Gap withheld**: full story behind the object.
