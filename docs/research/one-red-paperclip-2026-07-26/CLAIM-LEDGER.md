## Date Caution

Kyle's official retrospective says he started with the paperclip on July 12, 2005 and completed the final exchange on July 12, 2006. Secondary timelines sometimes place the first completed trade on July 14. The Short does not need that distinction; use `In 2005` and `One year`.

## Narration Guardrails

Allowed:

```text
Can this paperclip buy that house?
In 2005, Kyle MacDonald set one rule: trade up until somebody offered him a home.
Fourteen trades. One year.
Kipling, Saskatchewan wanted that role. The town traded Kyle a house.
```

Blocked:

```text
Every trade doubled his money.
He turned one cent into a hundred-thousand-dollar house.
He never spent a dollar on the project.
The house is worth a specific current amount.
Anyone can repeat this result.
```
