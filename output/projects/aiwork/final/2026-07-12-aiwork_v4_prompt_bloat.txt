Title: Why Adding More Instructions Makes Your AI Worse

Description:
A team building their own AI assistant kept adding instructions every time it needed a new capability - until the instructions ballooned to hundreds of lines and it started making mistakes it never made before.

The fix wasn't more instructions. It was breaking everything down into small, focused steps instead of one giant prompt trying to do everything at once.
