Title: Why Adding More Instructions Makes Your AI Worse

Description:
A team building their own AI assistant kept adding instructions every time it needed a new capability - until the instructions ballooned to 400 lines and it started making mistakes it never made before.

The fix wasn't more instructions. It was organizing everything into small, focused pieces the AI only pulls in when it actually needs them - cutting it down to 15 lines and fixing the mistakes at the same time.
