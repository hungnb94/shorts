TITLE:
She Sold Her House For This. Smart Or Reckless?

DESCRIPTION:
She sold her home for $310,000, took home $150,000 after paying off the remaining loan, and combined that equity with savings for a $200,000 down payment on a $300,000 laundromat. The seller financed the remaining $100,000 at 6% over two years.

In 2024, the laundromat brought in about $475,000 in revenue. CNBC reports $119,000 in business profit, while the owner says she paid herself $66,000. Those are three different numbers: revenue is not profit, and business profit is not owner pay.

She now works around five to six hours per week—but only after years of hiring employees and building systems. The real question isn't whether laundromats are passive. It's whether the purchase price, owner pay, and owner hours make the risk worth it.

Smart or reckless? Would you sell your house to buy a business?
