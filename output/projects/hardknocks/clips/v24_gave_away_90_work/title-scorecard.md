# HardKnocks V24 Title Scorecard

Scoring dimensions: cold-viewer clarity, stakes, open loop, factual accuracy and mobile-length compliance. Each dimension is worth 10 points.

| Family | Candidate | Visible characters | Score |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Money/number | `From 100% To 10% 💸🤯` | 19 | 43/50 |
| Decision | `He Sold 90% To Scale 📈🤯` | 23 | 44/50 |
| Contradiction | `10% Beat 100% 📉💰` | 16 | 43/50 |
| Authority | `Freshly Founder Sold 90% 💼💸` | 27 | 44/50 |
| Question | `Why He Sold 90% 📉🤔` | 18 | **47/50** |

The question title wins because it states the counterintuitive decision without claiming a personal payout or treating the entire `$1.5B` headline as unconditional consideration. It opens the same causal question as the frame-zero ownership reversal and is paid off by the people, capital and scale explanation.
