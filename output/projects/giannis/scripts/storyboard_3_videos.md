These are the people you got to trust.
These are the people that are going to handle everything in your life.

You will always say, oh, what is this? I don't understand this. This is X, Y, and Z.

They should never be boys and cool, man.
You know, because one, they can keep one another accountable if they don't know one each other.
Oh, that guy is doing X, Y, and Z wrong. Oh, this guy, your lawyer, you overlooked that.
