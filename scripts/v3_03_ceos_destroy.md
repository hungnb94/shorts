Why do most CEOs destroy their own companies?
It is not bad products.
It is not bad markets.
It is not even bad luck.
It is ego.
Founders win by listening to customers.
Then they scale.
They build a moat of yes-men.
They stop hearing the truth.
They optimize for the next quarter instead of the next decade.
And that is when the decay starts.
Blockbuster asked Netflix for a partnership in the year two thousand.
The CEO said no.
You know the rest.
Great CEOs die poor.
Great companies die rich.
The question is which one you want to be.
